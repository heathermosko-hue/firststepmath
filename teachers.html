💡Classroom Tips

Ways teachers and parents are using FirstStepMath to reinforce learning.

  • Morning Warm-Up Open FirstStepMath at a tech center. Students pick their grade and a topic from today's lesson as a 5-minute warm-up.
  • Targeted Practice After introducing a concept (e.g. Making Ten), assign that specific quiz topic so students get immediate repetition.
  • Math Tools Station Let students explore the built-in Math Toolbox independently — the same manipulatives used on your whiteboard, available on-screen.
  • Print & Go Worksheets Every quiz topic has a matching printable worksheet. Print a set to send home as homework that mirrors in-class practice.
  • Progress Motivation Students see their pretend money grow with every correct answer. The store visit is a natural reward at the end of a session.
  • Differentiation Struggling students can work on Pre-K or K topics. Advanced learners can move into Grade 1 topics — all from the same site.
